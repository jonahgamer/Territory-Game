    Problem map

    Click or drag to place and clear cells. On a phone, pinch the map to zoom and drag with one finger to slide it. With Problem selected, choose Math, OI, Science, or Puzzle, then place the cell. Click a problem cell to open that problem. The border shows difficulty. Spawn points are where a team can start.

    Each empty cell gets one saved problem. A problem that is not on this map is used first. If every match is already on the map, the one that appears least often is used.
