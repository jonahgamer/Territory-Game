    Schedule

    Teams are placed on random spawn points when the contest starts. There is no choosing period. The wait still runs before solving begins.

    Energy

    Each team shares one pool. The maximum is 100. During the solving period the clock restores energy, and each attempt spends energy. A correct answer also restores the difficulty amount while the team is below the line you set.
