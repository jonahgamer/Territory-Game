    Energy

    Each team shares one pool. The maximum is 100. During the solving period the clock restores energy, and each attempt spends energy. A correct answer also restores the difficulty amount while the team is below the line you set.

    Hidden rules

    Contestants do not see a hidden rule until its reveal time. A rule can stay hidden for the whole contest. This page chooses which rules this contest uses. Descriptions are edited on the hidden rule list.

      Teleport paths

      Connect two teleport cells. A pair can stay on one map or join two maps. The link belongs to this contest, so the map itself is unchanged. The lines on the contest page stay transparent, and a contestant can hide them.
